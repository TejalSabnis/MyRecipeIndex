# Creamy Garlic Pasta

**Servings:** 3
**Prep Time:** 10 min
**Cook Time:** 20 min

## Ingredients

- 12 oz fettuccine
- 4 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1.5 cups heavy cream
- 0.75 cup parmesan
- 1 pinch salt
- 1 pinch black pepper
- 2 tbsp parsley

## Instructions

1. Cook the pasta in salted boiling water until al dente, then drain, reserving 1/2 cup of pasta water.
2. Melt the butter in a large pan over medium heat and saute the minced garlic until fragrant, about 1 minute.
3. Pour in the heavy cream and bring to a gentle simmer.
4. Whisk in the parmesan until the sauce is smooth, adding pasta water as needed to loosen it.
5. Toss the cooked pasta in the sauce, season with salt and pepper, and garnish with parsley.

## Notes

Add grilled chicken or shrimp to make it a heartier meal.
