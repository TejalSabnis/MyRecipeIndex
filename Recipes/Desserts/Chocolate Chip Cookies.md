# Chocolate Chip Cookies

**Servings:** 24
**Prep Time:** 15 min
**Cook Time:** 12 min

## Ingredients

- 2.25 cups flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup butter
- 0.75 cup sugar
- 0.75 cup brown sugar
- 2 whole eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 cups chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. Whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt in a bowl and set aside.
3. Cream the softened butter with both sugars until light and fluffy.
4. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then stir in the vanilla.
5. Gradually mix in the flour mixture, then fold in the chocolate chips.
6. Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto a baking sheet and bake for 9-11 minutes until golden at the edges.

## Notes

Chill the dough for 30 minutes for thicker, chewier cookies.
