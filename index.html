Welcome to your Sweet Recipe Box!

This little app saves everything as plain markdown files right on your computer — no accounts, no cloud, no internet required once it's open. Pick a folder to get started (an empty one, or the sample Recipes folder that came with this app), and you can start creating categories and recipes.

Works best in Chrome, Edge, or Opera — these are the browsers that let a page talk to local files. Your files stay right where you put them; nothing is ever uploaded anywhere. 💕
